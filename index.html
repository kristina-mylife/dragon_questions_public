AI self-reflection companion

Better questions. Deeper self‑reflection.

Dragon Questions answers what is on your mind with one thoughtful, sometimes uncomfortable question instead of advice. It helps you look at your assumptions, notice the patterns you repeat, and find the angle you have been avoiding.

The product

A question that does the work advice can't

Most chat assistants rush to solve the problem you describe. People rarely change because someone told them what to do. They change when they notice something about their own thinking. Dragon Questions is built around that moment.

Inspired by coaching practice

The questions follow the style of a working coach: short, specific to what you just said, and aimed at the assumption underneath it. The product does not impersonate any particular person.

Not therapy

Dragon Questions is a reflection tool, not a medical or psychological service, and it does not replace professional help. When a conversation shows signs of risk, the bot moves into a gentler support mode and points to real help.

How it works

Three steps, as many rounds as you need

  1. Share what's on your mind

    Describe a situation, a challenge or a thought that keeps coming back, in your own words.

  2. Get a thoughtful question

    The bot replies with a single question meant to make you think, not with a list of tips.

  3. Explore your perspective

    Answer, keep going, and stop whenever you like. Many people find what they had overlooked a few questions in.

Technology

Built with open-weight AI

The questions come from our own fine-tuned model, not from a general-purpose chatbot behind a prompt. Quality is improved through structured evaluation and human review, round after round.

Base model
Qwen-based open-weight model (Qwen3.8, 27B), chosen so we can train it and control how it is served.
Fine-tuning
LoRA adapters trained on coach-written questions and reviewed dialogues, with a second version trained for multi-turn conversations without repeats.
Inference
vLLM on Modal serverless GPUs. Containers scale to zero between conversations, and a smaller hosted model covers cold starts.
Evaluation
Held-out dialogues, automatic checks for repeats and worn openings, and blind A/B review of model versions by a professional coach.
Safety routing
Every turn is checked for risk signals before the model answers; risky conversations switch to a support mode with fixed, careful replies.
Privacy
Separate consent for human review and for training. Reviewers see dialogues without usernames, and raw texts are deleted after a retention period.

Current stage

Early, live, and learning from real conversations

Dragon Questions has been open to a closed group of testers since September 2026. Usage is bursty: quiet days, then days when dozens of people talk to it at once. That pattern is exactly why the model runs on serverless GPUs.
